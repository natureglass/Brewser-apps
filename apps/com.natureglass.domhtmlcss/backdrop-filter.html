Frosted sticky bar backdrop-filter: blur(10px)

CSS backdrop-filter

The bar above and the pill at the bottom-right stay put while this page scrolls; the colours behind them blur and re-blur in real time. Backdrop pixels are read from the body's offscreen cache (never the Skia screen, whose read-back corrupts alpha). Moving elements use a fast drawImage-only blur (no per-frame pixel read-back), so colour functions apply on the baked cards below but not on the bar / pill.
