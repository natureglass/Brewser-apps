MATRIX LAYOUT

Two stacked 32×8 panels → width 32, height 16, panel height 8. Width × height is capped at 1024 LEDs. IDENTIFY marks the corners (red = top-left, green = top-right, blue = bottom-left) and sweeps left→right so you can dial in wiring/flips until the physical panel matches. RAW ignores all mapping and lights physical LEDs 0,1,2… in chain order with a colour band every 16 and white every 128 — use it to read how your panel is actually wired.

ESP32

Pin + LED count + brightness are pushed to the ESP32 on connect and on SAVE. Brightness is applied on-device (power limiting — 512 LEDs at full white pulls ~30 A).

CONNECTION

The app is fully functional offline; a link just mirrors the canvas to the panel. USB runs up to ~50 fps for 512 LEDs. BLE is paced by the panel's acknowledgement and lands around 1 frame/s for 512 LEDs (a Windows + Chrome platform limit) — use it for control and slow updates, USB for animation.
